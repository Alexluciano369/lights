Permanent Outdoor Lighting FAQ

Everything Bensalem homeowners ask us

How much do permanent LED lights cost in Bensalem?

Most Bensalem homes run $3,500–$8,500 installed depending on roofline complexity and linear footage. We give a firm on-site quote after measuring — no ballpark surprises.

Can I still use them for holidays besides Christmas?

Yes. Every color, every pattern, every holiday. Red-white-blue for the Fourth of July, orange for Halloween, green for St. Patrick's Day, team colors for game day, and a warm-white 'downlight' look for every other night.

Are the lights visible during the day?

The color-matched aluminum channel is nearly invisible from the street. Most neighbors don't notice the system until the first time it turns on.

Do they work in PA winters?

Yes. The RGBW LEDs and channel are engineered for outdoor use in freezing temperatures, ice, and heavy rain. All splices are weatherproof.

Is there a warranty?

The LED modules carry a multi-year manufacturer warranty, and our workmanship is warranted for life. If a section ever goes dark, we come back and fix it.

Gutter Guards FAQ

Everything Marlton homeowners ask us

How much do gutter guards cost in Marlton, NJ?

Most single-story homes in Marlton run $8–$14 per linear foot installed. Two-story homes with high pitches and complex rooflines range from $14–$22 per foot. We give a firm written quote on site, in writing, before any work starts.

Do LeafBlaster Pro guards really stop pine needles?

Yes. The 50-micron stainless-steel mesh is finer than a pine needle, so needles slide off the sloped surface instead of embedding. This is the #1 reason homeowners in the Pine Barrens choose LeafBlaster Pro over screen guards.

Will gutter guards work with my existing gutters?

In most cases, yes. We inspect your existing 5-inch or 6-inch aluminum gutters, resecure them, replace failing sections, and install the mesh on top. If gutters are undersized or damaged beyond repair, we quote seamless replacement in the same visit.

Is there a warranty?

LeafBlaster Pro carries a lifetime transferable no-clog performance warranty, and we back our install with a lifetime workmanship warranty. If it ever clogs on us, we come back and fix it — free.

How long does installation take in Marlton?

Most Marlton homes are completed in a single day. We arrive between 7:30 and 8:30 AM, take care of cleanout, install the guards, water-test everything, and clean the property before we leave.

Are you licensed and insured in NJ?

Yes. We carry full general liability insurance, workers' comp, and required NJ home-improvement contractor registration. We provide certificates on request before any deposit is taken.
